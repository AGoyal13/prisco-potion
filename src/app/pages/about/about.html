Our Story

A passion for exceptional beverages.

Prisco Potion Pvt. Ltd. was founded with a singular mission: bring the world's finest spirits and beverages to the Indian market, while crafting world-class labels of our own.

Based in South Delhi's Okhla Industrial Area, Prisco Potion Pvt. Ltd. has grown from a focused import business into a full-spectrum spirits company. We own our manufacturing through Exquisite Spirit and Beverages Pvt. Ltd., our in-house distillery, producing beers and whiskeys under our own labels.

Our international division curates and brings to India some of the world's most exciting spirits — from Tigre Blanc to VIVIR Tequila, Hepple Gin to Ignite — making premium international beverages accessible to the Indian connoisseur.

Distribution is the backbone of our reach. Our network spans Delhi and continues to expand into new cities and markets, ensuring our portfolio is available wherever quality is valued.
